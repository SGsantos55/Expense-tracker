Frequently Asked Questions

Click or tap a question to see the answer.

What is ExpenseTracker and is it really free?

ExpenseTracker is a free, browser-based app for logging expenses and seeing where your money goes. There is no subscription, no credit card and no setup required.

How do I track my spending?

Create a free account, then add each expense with its amount, category and date. The app groups and totals everything for you automatically, so there are no formulas or manual spreadsheets.

What kind of expenses can I track?

Any expense — food, travel, rent, bills, shopping, subscriptions and business costs — organized by category so you can see exactly where your money goes.

Can I use it for business or freelancing?

Yes. Log travel, supplies and software under their own categories so work spending stays separate from personal spending and is ready for tax season.

Do I need a spreadsheet or to install anything?

No. ExpenseTracker runs in any browser on desktop or mobile, so there is nothing to install and no spreadsheet formulas to maintain.

Why use an expense tracker instead of guessing?

Instant totals and monthly category breakdowns show exactly where your money goes, so you can cut waste, stick to a budget and save more.
