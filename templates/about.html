About Us

Track every rupee. Own your finances.

Who we are

ExpenseTracker is a free expense tracker app built to make managing money simple. We believe everyone deserves a clear picture of their spending without wrestling with fragile spreadsheets or paying for bloated software. Our tool lets you log expenses in seconds, organize them by category and instantly see where your money goes.

Our mission

Our mission is to help people, small businesses and self-employed workers take control of their finances. By turning everyday spending into clear monthly reports, we make it easier to build better habits, stick to a budget and save more of what you earn.

What you can do

  • Log personal, household and business expenses in one place
  • Organize spending into clear categories
  • See monthly totals and trends at a glance
  • Track your money for free — no subscription, no credit card
